About CMFI RWANDA

For over three decades, Grace Cathedral has been a beacon of faith, love, and community transformation. We are a multi-generational church dedicated to making disciples and extending God’s grace to all.

Our doors are open to everyone — regardless of background or belief. With vibrant worship, deep biblical teaching, and life-changing outreach, we exist to lead people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ.
